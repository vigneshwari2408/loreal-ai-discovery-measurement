Chapter 01 · Digital Channel Performance

L'Oréal's own digital channel
never moved in a straight line.

Five years of L'Oréal's own public disclosures — the company's most closely measured channel — already resist a tidy narrative. That's the baseline for everything that follows.

E-commerce Share of Sales

Fell 1.9 pp between 2021–2023, then recovered to 28.2% in 2024 and reached 30.2% in 2025.

The decline did not mean e-commerce sales were falling — total L'Oréal sales were simply growing faster in 2022 and 2023.

Source: L'Oréal Annual Reports, 2021–2024; L'Oréal 2025 Universal Registration Document.

Growth vs. Margin

LFL growth fell from 16.1% to 4.0%, while margin rose from 19.1% to 20.2%.
Two metrics moved in different directions — this does not establish that one caused the other.

Chapter 02 · The Central Distinction

Attribution ≠ Incrementality

A purchase tagged "AI-referred" is not proof AI caused it. Click each path.

Attribution

AI Discovery→ Referral→ Retailer Visit→ Purchase✓
This purchase happened, and a tracking signal linked it to an AI referral. That's a fact about the tag — not yet a fact about causation.

Incrementality

AI Discovery→ Referral→ Retailer Visit→ Purchase?
This purchase might have happened even without the AI referral. Attribution alone can't tell you — only a comparison group can.
A tracking signal can attribute a conversion; a comparison-group test is needed to estimate incremental lift.
Chapter 02 · Measurement Readiness

What would L'Oréal need
to measure this credibly?

Estimating a credible minimum detectable effect (MDE) depends on data and design inputs — here's which ones public data can and can't provide.

Market-level outcome variance
● Not publicly available
Estimates the natural outcome variability an effect must be distinguished from.
Eligible comparable test/control markets
● Not publicly available
Determines the feasible sample size and comparison structure.
Baseline outcome level (e.g. market-level sales)
● Not publicly available
Defines the scale against which an incremental lift is evaluated.
Sufficient pre-test market history
● Not publicly available
Captures baseline trends and seasonality before the intervention.
Test duration
● Decidable in advance
Determines the observation window and contributes to detectable effect size.

No L'Oréal-specific MDE can be calculated from public data. The target effect, alpha, and statistical power should be pre-specified as part of the experiment design.

Chapter 03 · What I Would Test First

A feasibility-gated pilot
for a live ad placement

L'Oréal brands have been running paid ChatGPT ads since April 2026. This is how I'd design the measurement layer for it — checking whether the test is possible before assuming it is.

01
Feasibility Check
Confirm whether L'Oréal's specific ChatGPT ad placement — not just the platform's general capability — supports the market-level targeting and reporting required for the test.
If the required market-level assignment or reporting is unavailable for this partnership specifically: establish the measurement arrangement before running the test.
02
Test + Control Markets
Test markets with paid ChatGPT placement active vs. matched control markets without it.
Market count should be determined from the pre-test power calculation and the eligible market pool.
03
Incremental Sales Lift
Compare pre/post sales changes in exposed markets against matched controls. Primary metric: Incremental Sales Lift.
Inconclusive / no effect if statistical power is insufficient — redesign the test; do not call it zero impact.
04
Apply Decision Rule
Predefine the minimum business-relevant lift and decision rule; use the pre-test power calculation to determine whether the design can detect it.
Data required: sufficient pre-test market history · confirmed placement eligibility/assignment · platform/retailer measurement access.
SELF-CORRECTION

I initially framed the pilot around a geo-holdout test. I then checked whether public evidence established the targeting and reporting needed to run one. It didn't — so feasibility became Stage 1 rather than an assumption.
